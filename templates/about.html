Examinations, reimagined.

Testify brings classes, exams, student enrollment, and results into one calm, professional platform — so educators can focus on teaching and students can focus on performing.

Vision

A calmer way to examine

We envision a world where running an online exam feels as simple as uploading a file — where teachers spend their time on teaching, not on fighting tools, and students get a focused, fair experience on exam day.

Mission

From CSV to results, effortlessly

Our mission is to connect class management, enrollment, exam creation, and result reporting into one continuous workflow — powered by CSV enrollment and a clean, responsive interface for every role.

Why Testify

Built around real examination workflows

CSV-first enrollment

Teachers upload a single CSV to enroll an entire class — no manual student entry, no separate student database.

Timed exam interface

A countdown timer, question palette, and mark-for-review keep students focused and exams fair.

Result CSV reporting

Upload, preview, search, and sort result CSVs, then generate leaderboards and reports instantly.

Role-based dashboards

Admin, teacher, and student each get a tailored interface with exactly the tools they need.

Fully responsive

From desktop to mobile, every page — including the exam screen — adapts to any device.

Secure by design

Enrollment-based student login and role-based access keep the right people in the right places.

How roles fit together

  • Publish & close exams

  • Take timed exams
  • Navigate questions
